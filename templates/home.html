About SellSense AI

We provide a simple and reliable platform for business owners and staff to monitor daily operations, evaluate performance trends, and improve sales outcomes with less guesswork.

SELLSENSE-AI

Professional Multi-Business Sales Management Platform

Overview

SELLSENSE-AI is a secure Django-powered sales management system designed to support multiple businesses on a single platform while keeping every business's data completely isolated and protected.

The platform helps businesses manage sales, inventory, employees, analytics, reports, and revenue tracking from one centralized dashboard.

Key Features

🏢 Multi-Business Architecture
  • Multiple businesses on one platform
  • Business-level data isolation
  • Secure access controls
  • Cross-business protection
🔐 Authentication & Security
  • Secure login system
  • Password hashing
  • Account activation/deactivation
  • Password reset management
💰 Sales Management
  • Record daily sales
  • Track revenue
  • Manage products and prices
  • View sales history
👥 Employee Management
  • Employer dashboard
  • Employee account management
  • Role-based permissions
  • Password reset functionality
📊 Reports & Analytics
  • Daily, Weekly & Monthly reports
  • Revenue summaries
  • Revenue charts
  • Business statistics dashboard
📄 PDF Generation
  • Download sales reports
  • Revenue summaries
  • Professional PDF exports
  • Printable business reports

Data Protection

  • Secure business-level data isolation
  • Protected report storage
  • Automatic deletion of reports older than one year
  • Django-powered security architecture
  • Scalable multi-tenant system design
